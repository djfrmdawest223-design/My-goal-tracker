📖 The Story

I didn't start with thousands of dollars. I started around $1,000 a month. I had bills, a spending problem when money sat on my card, and a dream that looked much bigger than where I was. Then I stopped waiting for everything to be perfect. October 2026 became my setup month. I covered my essentials, built a cushion, and prepared for November 2. November 2 was the start of the next chapter. I didn't need to make the whole $60,000 in one day. I needed to win one day at a time. My long-term DoorDash target became around $240/day gross. I learned to track real dollars per hour because working longer isn't the only answer. Better orders and better hours matter too. Every day I track the number. $20. $60. $100. $150. $200. $240. The number grows. The Hellcat target is July 28, 2027. After the Hellcat, the dream gets bigger. Texas target: July 28, 2028. Long-term income target: around $7,000/month. A possible house-fund target is $3,000–$3,500/month when the budget allows. I'm not building everything in one day. I'm building it one day at a time. HELLCAT FIRST. TEXAS NEXT. I'M STILL BUILDING.

💡 Money Rule

Bills money has a job. Spending money has a job. Savings has a job. Emergency money has a job.
